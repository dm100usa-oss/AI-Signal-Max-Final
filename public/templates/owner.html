Introduction

This report has been prepared for the website owner. It shows the current condition of your site in terms of visibility across AI platforms and explains which factors have the greatest impact. The results are summarized first, followed by detailed explanations and recommendations. The final section of this report contains a developer’s checklist that can be handed over directly for implementation.

Checked Parameters

Robots.txt
{{status_robots}}

This file determines whether AI systems can access your website. If it is misconfigured or blocks access, your entire site may disappear from AI-driven results.

Sitemap.xml
{{status_sitemap}}

A sitemap tells AI which pages exist on your website and which are most important for indexing.

X-Robots-Tag
{{status_xrobots}}

These server-side headers signal whether AI can display your pages.

Meta Robots
{{status_meta}}

This is a meta tag placed in the page’s HTML that also controls whether AI can index it.

Canonical
{{status_canonical}}

A canonical tag tells AI which version of a page is the “master” copy.

Title Tag
{{status_title}}

The page title is the first element both customers and AI systems see.

Meta Description
{{status_metadesc}}

A meta description provides a short preview of your page content beneath the title in results.

Open Graph Tags
{{status_og}}

These tags control how your links appear in social media, messengers, and AI-generated answers.

H1 Headings
{{status_h1}}

The H1 tag is the main headline of a page.

Structured Data
{{status_schema}}

Structured data markup helps AI understand exactly what is on your site.

Mobile Friendly
{{status_mobile}}

Today, most visitors access websites from mobile devices.

HTTPS Security
{{status_https}}

A secure HTTPS connection is a basic trust signal.

Alt Attributes
{{status_alt}}

Alt texts are descriptions of images that allow AI systems to “see” visual content.

Favicon
{{status_favicon}}

The favicon is the small icon displayed in browsers and results.

Custom 404 Page
{{status_404}}

A 404 error page tells AI and users that a page doesn’t exist.
